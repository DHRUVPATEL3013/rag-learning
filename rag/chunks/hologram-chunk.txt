Employee Attendance Policy
Working Hours
Employees are expected to work 8 hours per working day, Monday through Friday.
Office Arrival
Employees should arrive at the office by 9:30 AM unless they have an approved flexible-work
arrangement.
Late Arrival
Employees arriving more than 30 minutes late should inform their manager and record the reason in the
attendance system.
Remote Work
Employees working remotely must remain available during the agreed working hours and attend
scheduled meetings.
